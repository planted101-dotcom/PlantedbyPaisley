Policies

The questions I get most

Do I need to be home?

No. I just need access to the yard and to a spigot if the job calls for water. Plenty of clients have never met me in person, and that is completely fine.

How far out are you booking?

Usually about two weeks. If something opens up sooner I will let you know, and recurring clients hold a standing slot so they are never in the queue.

What happens if it rains?

Weather moves things around, especially in spring. If I have to reschedule I will text you as early as I can and we will find the next good day.

What if I need to cancel?

Just give me at least 24 hours notice and there is no charge. Short notice cancellations are hard to fill, so please text as soon as you know.

Is there a minimum?

One hour per visit. Most jobs run longer than that anyway, but it means very small tasks are worth grouping together.

Do you haul away what you cut?

Yes, unless you would rather keep it for compost. If a job produces enough debris to need a dump run, I will tell you what that adds before I load anything.

Please secure pets before I arrive

Dogs, cats, and anything else with paws need to be inside or fenced away from the area I am working in. Mowers and trimmers are loud and unpredictable, gates end up propped open while I haul, and I would rather nobody's animal has a bad day. I always close gates behind me, but I cannot be responsible for a pet loose in the yard while the work is happening. If that is tricky for your setup, text me and we will plan around it.
